Frequently asked questions

Is the simulator free?

Yes. It's free to use, with no account and no download. It runs in any modern web browser.

Does it produce real electrical stimulation?

No. It's software only and doesn't connect to any hardware. The output monitor shows an illustration of each waveform family, not a measured electrical signal.

How accurate is it?

Screens, parameter ranges and button behaviour follow the Vectra Genisys user manual, the service manual and walkthrough videos of the real unit. Some details aren't documented anywhere, such as the exact settings that each Indication loads, so those are approximations. The accuracy page lists every one, and the device reference lists what the sources confirm. Always follow your instructor, your clinic's protocols and the manufacturer's documentation when treating patients.

Does it simulate ultrasound, sEMG or combination therapy?

Those menus are on the Home screen, but the simulator focuses on electrotherapy. Ultrasound and Combination report that no applicator is connected, as the real unit does without one, and sEMG isn't simulated.

Does it work on a phone or offline?

Yes. It runs on phones and tablets as well as desktops, and you can use touch, a mouse or a keyboard. After your first visit it also works offline, and you can add it to your home screen to use it like an app.

Is it made by Chattanooga or Enovis?

No. It's an unofficial project for training and exploration, and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Enovis, DJO or Chattanooga.
