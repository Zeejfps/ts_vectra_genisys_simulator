About the Vectra Genisys Simulator

A free, interactive replica of the Chattanooga Vectra Genisys electrotherapy unit's interface that runs in your web browser.

It reproduces the unit's colour screen, its ten soft keys, the Home, Back and Clinical Resources Library buttons, the Start, Pause and Stop buttons, and the intensity knob. It also follows the screen flow described in the Vectra Genisys user manual, so you can learn where each setting lives and how a treatment is set up before you use a real unit.

An output monitor next to the unit draws the shape of the selected waveform as you change its parameters. It shows how carrier frequency, beat frequency, phase duration, burst rate, ramp and cycle time change the output.

Who it's for

  • Physical therapy (PT) and physical therapist assistant (PTA) students learning therapeutic modalities.
  • Athletic training, chiropractic and occupational therapy students who work with electrical stimulation (e-stim).
  • Instructors who want to demonstrate the interface in class or assign practice outside the lab.
  • New clinicians getting to know a Vectra Genisys before using one with patients.

Electrotherapy waveforms you can practice

All ten electrotherapy waveforms on the unit are simulated, with the parameter ranges from the user manual. The device reference has the full ranges and defaults.

Interferential current (IFC, 4 pole)
Carrier of 2500, 4000 or 5000 Hz, beat frequency of 1–200 Hz with optional sweep, and vector scan. Up to 100 mA (CC) or 100 V (CV).
Premodulated (IFC, 2 pole)
A fixed 2500 Hz carrier with the same beat frequency and sweep options as four-pole IFC.
Asymmetrical biphasic
Phase duration of 20–1000 µs, 1–250 Hz, with burst, frequency modulation and amplitude modulation. Up to 110 mA.
Symmetrical biphasic
The same parameters as asymmetrical biphasic, with a balanced waveform, up to 80 mA.
VMS and VMS Burst
Single, reciprocal and co-contract channel modes, phase duration of 20–400 µs, 1–200 pps, ramp, cycle time and anti-fatigue. Up to 200 mA.
Russian stimulation
A 2500 Hz carrier with a 10–50% duty cycle and 20–100 bursts per second, commonly used for muscle strengthening protocols.
Microcurrent
5–1000 µA with positive, negative or alternating polarity, delivered through pads or a probe.
High voltage pulsed current (HVPC)
Up to 500 V, with polarity, sweep and 10–120 pps. The display can show volts or peak current.
Direct current (DC)
Up to 4 mA, with polarity reversal halfway through the treatment and 1–10 minute treatment times.

What you can do

  • Set up a treatment on channel 1 or 2, edit its parameters, then start, pause and stop it with the timer running.
  • Switch between constant current (CC) and constant voltage (CV) and read the intensity in the status area.
  • Choose a waveform from the Electrotherapy Indications screen and browse Clinical Protocols by body area in the Clinical Resources Library.
  • Press the Patient Interrupt Switch and see how the unit responds.
  • Open Operator Utilities (Home and Back together) to set the clinic name, volume, date and time, and pad contact quality.
  • Speed up the treatment timer to 10× or 60× to see a full session end without waiting.

The simulator works with a mouse, a touch screen or a keyboard, and runs on desktop, tablet and phone. You can install it as an app and use it offline.
