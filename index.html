A free Vectra Genisys trainer in your browser

This is an interactive replica of the Chattanooga Vectra Genisys electrotherapy unit's interface. It reproduces the colour screen, the ten soft keys, the Home, Back and Clinical Resources Library buttons, Start, Pause and Stop, and the intensity knob, and follows the screen flow in the user manual. Practice IFC, Russian, biphasic, VMS, high volt, microcurrent and DC treatment setup before you use a real unit. It works on desktop, tablet and phone.
