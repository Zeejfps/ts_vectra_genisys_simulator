How to use the Vectra Genisys Simulator

Set up and run a treatment on the simulated unit with a mouse, a touch screen or a keyboard.

Open the simulator

Getting started

  1. Turn the unit on with the power switch at the top left (the real switch is on the back panel).
  2. Press Electrotherapy, then a waveform. Channels you set up show in the yellow status area.
  3. Edit changes parameters. Pressing a setting cycles its options. Number settings open an editor that uses the arrow keys.
  4. Turn the intensity knob by dragging it, scrolling over it, or using the ↑/↓ keys.
  5. Start, Pause and Stop control the framed channel. Select Channel on Home moves the frame.
  6. Press Home and Back together to open Operator Utilities.

Simulator controls

These buttons float over the corners of the unit: power at the top left, timer speed at the top right, the patient interrupt switch at the bottom left, and zoom at the bottom right.

Power switch
Turns the unit on and off. Turning it off ends any treatment.
Timer speed (clock)
Each press cycles the treatment timer through 1×, 10× and 60×, so you can see a full session end without waiting.
Patient Interrupt Switch (red stop sign)
Presses the hand-held switch a patient holds during treatment. It pauses running treatments on channels 1 and 2 and shows a message on screen.
Zoom (− 100% +)
Enlarges the unit without zooming the rest of the page. You can also pinch on the unit with two fingers or a trackpad. Once zoomed in, drag or scroll to move around. Press the percentage to fit the whole unit again.
